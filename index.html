Portfolio

Undergraduate final-year research project

For the final-year capstone project, my project partner and I designed and developed a mobile health (mHealth) application to improve tuberculosis (TB) screening and monitoring in rural communities of Eswatini. The app, built with Flutter (frontend) and Firebase (backend), includes a digital TB symptom screening tool, referral system with geolocation and multilingual educational content. We selected this project seeing its potential to support early TB detection, improve healthcare access, and serve as a model for addressing other public health challenges in low-resource settings.

Community Involvement

In July 2025, I was selected as the top student in my program to attend the Unlock Summit 2025 in Eswatini. Learning from leaders like Vusi Thembekwayo was an inspiring experience that reshaped how I see leadership and reinforced my drive to use technology for meaningful impact.

In October 2024, I represented the University of Eswatini at the Cyber 9/12 Strategy Challenge in Cape Town, a global cyber policy and strategy competition. Working in collaboration with my teammates under Team UNESWA, we tackled a simulated cyber crisis, and it sharpened my problem-solving, teamwork, and communication skills while fueling my interest in cybersecurity.

From 2024 to 2025, I served as Lead of Google Developer Groups on Campus (GDGoC) at UNESWA, guiding the chapter’s second term. I worked with a core team to raise tech awareness, host hands-on workshops, and create a space for students to learn and build together. Highlights included organizing a Hacktoberfest open-source contribution event, which encouraged students to actively engage with global tech communities.

In late 2023, I joined as part of the core team of the inaugural Google Developer Students Club(GDSC) - University Of Eswatini, where I served as the Community Manager. I was involved in organized numerous events including Build with AI, Career Development sessions and Info Sessions with various industry professionals.

In mid-2023, I became a member of the UNESWA ICT Society. I was enticed by their vision to create a space for tech minds to learn, innovate and collaborate. I assumed the role of UI Designer in the Society’s Project Management team, where I worked with my colleagues to craft user interfaces for the Society’s projects.

As much as believe in absorbing knowledge, I also like imparting it to others. From late 2023 to mid 2024, I worked with Eswatini Coding Bootcamp to teach a variety of tech skills to people interested in venturing in tech. My responsibilities are teaching Python and front-end web development to beginners.
